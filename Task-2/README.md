# 📊 Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

This Power BI project visualizes and analyzes sales and profit performance for a global superstore dataset. It includes key business metrics, interactive visuals, and actionable insights to help stakeholders make informed decisions.


## 📌 Dashboard Features

- **KPIs**: Total Sales, Total Profit
- **Sales by Category & Sub-Category** (Stacked Column Chart)
- **Profit by Product Name** (Horizontal Bar Chart)
- **Sales vs Profit by Region** (Map Visual)
- **Top & Bottom 10 Products by Profit**
- **Interactive Slicer**: Filter visuals by **Order Date**

---

## 🎯 Business Insights

- 📈 Office Supplies had the highest sales among sub-categories.
- ❗ Some products generated high sales but low profit (or even loss).
- 🌍 Western and Central regions contributed most to profit.
- 📦 Product-level analysis helps identify low-performing SKUs.
